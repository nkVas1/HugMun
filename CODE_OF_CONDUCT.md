# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Argue with the work, not the
person.

## Specifics that matter here

**Disagree with evidence, not with volume.** This project makes claims about human
cognition. If you think an interpretation in the research dossier is wrong, say so and
bring the sources. That is not conflict; it is the point.

**Do not talk about the users the way the industry usually does.** The people this app
is for are old. They are not "non-technical", not "silver surfers", not a challenge to be
designed around. Language that treats them as less than fully adult is not acceptable in
issues, commits, code comments or the product itself.

**Health claims are a safety matter.** Overstating what the app does is not enthusiasm;
it is a defect with a person on the other end of it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, publishing
others' private information, or unwelcome attention of any kind.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, or block contributors, at their
discretion.
